Getting there

By BTS Skytrain

Siam station (Sukhumvit and Silom lines), exit 2 or 6, then about 10 minutes on foot south along Henri Dunant Road, or a 5-minute taxi ride. Siam is the interchange of both BTS lines, so it is reachable from anywhere on the network.

By MRT Subway

Sam Yan station (Blue line), exit 2, about 10 minutes on foot through the Chulalongkorn campus. Convenient from Hua Lamphong railway station and the Silom, Sukhumvit and Ratchada areas.

By taxi or Grab

Ask for the Faculty of Dentistry, Chulalongkorn University on Henri Dunant Road, or show the Thai address above. Set the Grab destination to "Faculty of Dentistry Chulalongkorn University". Allow extra time on weekday mornings.

From the airports

Suvarnabhumi (BKK): Airport Rail Link to Phaya Thai, then BTS to Siam, about 50 minutes; taxi about 45 to 75 minutes, roughly 350 to 450 baht with tolls.
Don Mueang (DMK): taxi about 45 to 60 minutes, roughly 300 to 400 baht, or SRT Dark Red line to Bang Sue then MRT to Sam Yan.

By car and parking

Limited visitor parking is available in the faculty car park on Henri Dunant Road; please arrive early. Public parking is also available at Siam Square One, MBK Center and Samyan Mitrtown, each about 10 minutes on foot.

From other provinces

Trains arrive at Krung Thep Aphiwat (Bang Sue) station, then MRT to Sam Yan in about 20 minutes. Long-distance buses arrive at Mo Chit, Ekkamai or Sai Tai Mai terminals, each within an hour by BTS or taxi.

Where to stay

Nearby areas

The venue sits between Siam and Sam Yan. Hotels around BTS Siam, National Stadium and Ratchathewi, or MRT Sam Yan and Hua Lamphong, are all within a short walk or one station. Budget rooms cluster on Soi Kasemsan 1 opposite MBK and near Hua Lamphong; larger hotels are around Siam Square.
