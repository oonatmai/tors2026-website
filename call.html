Case Presentations

The scientific programme is built around clinical cases. Every orthodontic residency programme in Thailand is invited to present one case, chosen by the programme, and to discuss it with residents and faculty from the other universities.

One case per university

Each of the eight residency programmes selects one clinical case. The presenter is usually a resident, supported by the supervising faculty member. Please agree the case and the presenter within your department before registering.

Format

15-minute oral presentation followed by 15 minutes of discussion led by a panel of senior orthodontists. Slides in 16:9, in English. Diagnosis, treatment objectives, mechanics, results and critical appraisal are all welcome.

Certificates

Presenters receive a Certificate of Presentation naming the case title, in addition to the Certificate of Participation. Both are emailed after the symposium.

Presentation topics

Each case is registered under one of the following topics. The registration form shows how many presenters have already chosen each topic.

  • Skeletal Class II (non-surgical)
  • Skeletal Class III (non-surgical)
  • Orthognathic surgery
  • Clear aligner treatment
  • Growth modification / early treatment
  • Interdisciplinary treatment
  • Syndromic cases
  • TMD / TMJ-related orthodontic treatment
  • Asymmetry (non-surgical)
  • Other

How to register a case

  1. The presenting resident registers, chooses Case presenter and picks the topic of the case. The form shows which universities have already registered a case and how many presenters have chosen each topic. The case title can be given at registration or added later.
  2. Only one case presenter per university can be registered; a second registration from the same university is refused automatically.
  3. The case title is printed on the certificate and in the programme; presenters can edit it on their private presenter page until the deadline.
  4. Deadline for registration, case titles and slide upload: . Supervisors and co-presenters register separately as participants.

No abstract submission is required. Patient information must be anonymised in all slides.
