About the symposium

Who it is for

Orthodontic residents, postgraduate students and faculty members from the eight Thai universities with orthodontic residency programmes. Everyone is welcome to attend.

One university, one case

Each residency programme presents one clinical case in a 15-minute oral presentation, followed by discussion with a panel of senior orthodontists. Presenters receive a Certificate of Presentation.

Certificates

All registered participants receive a Certificate of Participation by email after the symposium, issued in the name and affiliation given at registration.
